<h1 align="center">Florence</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Smart Investment Assistant</strong> — Real-time market data, AI-powered analysis and virtual portfolio management.
</p>

---

Florence is a smart investment assistant that lets you track stocks, currency rates, precious metals and macroeconomic indicators on a single screen. It combines real-time market data with AI-powered analysis, allowing you to run strategy simulations, generate automated reports and manage virtual portfolios.

## Features

- **Market Dashboard** — Stocks, currency rates, precious metals and macroeconomic indicators in real time
- **AI Advisor** — Smart recommendations based on your portfolio and risk profile
- **Interactive Charts** — Candlestick charts with multiple timeframes and ranges
- **Strategy Simulations** — Test your strategies under different market scenarios
- **Virtual Portfolios** — Paper trading with commission accounting and XU100 benchmark
- **AI Reports** — Fundamental analysis, news sentiment and strategy evaluation reports
- **Data Export** — Google Takeout-style yearly data export
- **Watchlist and IPOs** — Favorite stocks and the latest IPO calendar

## Platforms

Use Florence in your browser, or access it through the [desktop](https://github.com/project-florence/desktop) and [mobile](https://github.com/project-florence/mobile) applications.
