Florence is a smart investment assistant that combines real-time market data, AI-driven analysis, and virtual portfolio management. Track stocks, currencies, precious metals, and macroeconomic indicators in one place; run simulations, generate automated reports, and manage virtual portfolios.

## Features

- **📊 Market Dashboard** — Real-time stock prices, currency rates, precious metals, and macroeconomic indicators
- **🤖 AI Advisor** — Smart recommendations based on your portfolio and risk profile
- **📈 Interactive Charts** — Candlestick charts with multiple timeframes and intervals
- **📄 Automated Reports** — Fundamental analysis, news sentiment, and strategy evaluation reports
- **🎮 Market Simulations** — Stress-test your strategies with advanced simulation models
- **💰 Virtual Portfolios** — Create portfolios and benchmark performance against XU100
- **📋 Watchlist** — Track your favorite stocks with real-time price updates
- **📢 Announcements** — Admin announcements and real-time notifications
- **🌐 Multilingual Interface** — Turkish and English support
- **🎨 Themes** — 6 handcrafted themes (Florence, Ocean, Emerald, Midnight, Sunset, Light)
